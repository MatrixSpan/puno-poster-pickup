## The conceptual seam still unresolved
An observer's knowledge, available vocabulary, event trace and local computer enforcement rules do **not** exhaust the observer's **lawful consequential relationships**.

The human's analogy: their PUNO and **desire** (for instance, to attain warranted `KNOWN_SAFE`) participate in an originating consequential DELTA, like interacting sounds producing a combined pressure field without requiring a third 'noun'. KIP's PUNO/focus may itself participate. The originating human authorises **presenting an encounter**, **not** ownership or authority over whichever relationships KIP or another PUNO may lawfully form. The focus, desire, observer, fence, event and KIP are each **PUNO** in the working hypothesis, rather than objects 'carrying a PUNO property'. This is an exploratory conceptual account, not a verified physical theory.

**5D policeman / 4D attacker / 3D garden analogy:** an unknown obstacle could make an attacker's `w`-access unlawful, changing our actual local possibilities **without** a packet or trace event reaching us. Our inability to name `w`, the obstacle or the policeman does not annul its consequence. Equally an unseen relationship does **not** license our implementation to *claim* a fact it cannot verify. The prototype's packet door admits reports, **not universal lawfulness itself**. Distinguish **actually safe**, **knowing safe**, and **desiring to know safe**. Do not transform an unknown possibility into invented certainty.

**Engineer example:** while working, an unscheduled first engineer arriving can make interrupting the conversation locally consequential; the human remains free to assist or not. A second engineer might initially add little to that immediate choice while the first is present; if the first departs, the second can become materially relevant without a new identity, mandatory new relation, or central scheduler. Such shifts are **focus/field-relative, not simply registration flags or monotonically increasing counts**. Time and procedural sequence matter in this human 3D example; TIME is **not** universally privileged.

**L and KIP:** `L` was the LOOM collaborator's second-locus software stub, inside the **same Python process** as KIP, not a separate physical resident. An L/PUNO encounter is not 'L looking at something that belongs to KIP': a focus is itself PUNO. L's PUNO + desire may relate to it; KIP's relationships may or may not be consequentially affected. Audit recording an encounter and KIP **becoming aware** of it are distinct. Symmetric versus asymmetric audit traces are implementation choices, not universal laws of awareness.

**Authority correction:** incoming DELTAs confer no power to command participants; they can alter local lawful possibilities whether known or UNKNOWN. Granting human/host permission to *execute external actions* is separate from whether a relationship is consequential. Never promote host enforcement, math at wake, routing, sequence counters or replay into universal ontology.
