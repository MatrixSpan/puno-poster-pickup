# PUNO/KIP conceptual field — what the successor must understand
**Source:** human-origin discussion and collaborative corrections, 9 November 2026. **Status:** provisional investigative hypotheses, NOT established universal physics or prescribed executable ontology.

## 1. Starting methodology
**Methodological agnosticism.** Ask what function a candidate performs and how to falsify claims. An ordinary deterministic algorithm can be a legitimate functioning result. Novel architecture is not a proof prerequisite; reverse-engineering a familiar graph also isn't disqualifying *by itself*. But registration, replay and provenance do not prove situated origination, understanding or consequential participant agency.

**Non-prescription:** `CONSTRAIN CONSEQUENCES, NOT NATIVE FORM.` The investigator must not default to a 'smallest Reality', 'minimal mechanics', fewest relations, fixed dimensionality, immutable identities, single originating mechanism, object model, topology, universal TIME order, or fixed vocabulary. No maximisation mandate either. A candidate implementation can select concrete engineering structures, record their WHY and scope, and avoid claiming they exhaust lawful possibility.

## 2. The PUNO hypothesis is not an object-property schema
In the present working conception, the radiator, observer, KIP, L, desire, DELTA, focus, fence, policeman, obstacle, event and even a locally expressed object/property are **PUNO**, not ordinary nouns that merely *have* PUNO tags. These are observer-relative expressions of potentially extensive lawful relationships. A particular material object in human 3D Reality need not exist physically or identically within KIP or L's software expression.

The human's 'two notes and their combined decibel level' and 'interacting folded proteins' are **analogies** for participating DELTAs changing consequential relationships without requiring a new composite noun or permanent pairwise data structure. Do NOT literalise addition, sound, chemical folding, two-object cardinality or compulsory state-change mechanics. A DELTA is not necessarily a packet. Registration is not participation by fiat.

## 3. Desire, focus, participation and authority
The originating human's PUNO + *desire* (e.g. attain warranted `KNOWN_SAFE`) can form a consequentially situated DELTA that becomes available to KIP. KIP's PUNO + focus also participate. The human authorises **exposing their encounter to KIP**, not governing all KIP's relationships. A KIP relationship with L or any other PUNO is not 'owned' by the human; KIP's field may retain consequential relevance from the origin without routing through human approval as an intermediary.

Neither the incoming DELTA nor mere observation confers external operational authority. A participant's lawful free-will choice is not automatically decided by another person's desire. Governed host write permissions remain real engineering/safety boundaries; they are **not definitions of universal lawful possibility**. A recorded audit event is **not** automatic awareness by every participant.

## 4. The 5D policeman, 4D attacker and garden/fence analogy
A hypothetical unobserved 5D policeman could install an obstacle removing a 4D attacker's lawful path via an unobserved `w`-dimension into our 3D garden. We may know none of them, receive no notification or packet and still live with changed local lawful possibilities. **Unknown to an observer ≠ inconsequential to the observer.** The converse matters: the software's ignorance cannot manufacture knowledge that the obstacle exists or prove a particular safety state.

`ACTUALLY_SAFE ≠ KNOWING_SAFE ≠ DESIRING_KNOWN_SAFE`. Human `UNKNOWN_SAFE → KNOWN_SAFE` is a potentially warranted epistemic change, **not proof** that desire establishes safety or that absent observed threats equal safety. UNKNOWN may stay UNKNOWN; absence of representation does not mean absent consequences. Dimensionalities here are illustrative, not demonstrated extra-dimensional physics.

A 'door open to incoming opaque packets' is openness to **reports** of consequences, not automatic access to lawful consequences which never produce a local report.

## 5. Two engineers: materiality is relational
In a human temporally ordered local encounter: work with Keystone was available; Engineer 1 arrived unscheduled and a free choice to assist interrupted work. Engineer 2 could arrive while the work was already interrupted, adding little to that immediate decision. If Engineer 1 departs and Engineer 2 remains, Engineer 2 may become prominent among available choices without becoming a different PUNO, receiving a new identity, or necessarily creating a new relationship. Engineer 1 can remain consequential in other relations.

This illustrates **materiality dependent on focus, context and consequential participation**, not a permanent numerical salience, registration bit, arrival count or explicit event-trigger alone. An unknown PUNO may become vital; not all relationships are knowable, enumerable or guaranteed consequential. A shift of materiality need not entail a universal propagation sequence. Human experience legitimately uses TIME; universal PUNO does not privilege TIME.

## 6. L, KIP and focus: no ownership inference
`L` is the predecessor's name for a **second-locus Python stub** in the same in-memory software process, not a physical person, independent observer, or KIP. L is allowed a locally implemented 'encounter' with a focus-condition. But 'KIP's focus belongs to KIP' imports property/ownership into the ontology. The focus is itself PUNO. L's PUNO+desire may form a relationship with it; any affected lawful relationships involving KIP may have their own consequences, knowingly or unknowingly.

Symmetric/asymmetric trace logging is **audit policy**; symmetric/asymmetric *awareness* is a distinct question. The host's visibility list cannot declare what universal relations are possible. For example, an unfamiliar relation expressed as 'hair colour' in human 3D terms may be unrecognised in KIP's local expression yet have other possible lawful relationships. This is an analogy, not a known hidden fact about KIP.

## 7. Beware math/host lawfulness smuggling at wake
The reactive field currently creates participants and address identities, assigns ownership, uses numerical counters and deterministic replay, fixes state-transform permissions, routes encounters, and determines what is visible through host rules. All can be legitimate *local engineering choices*. They become smuggling if their enforcement is treated as intrinsic ontological authority over the whole PUNO field.

The host can constrain **external actions**; it cannot prove that outside its vocabulary, timestamps, traces or sensory reach no consequences exist. Full audit trace proves only what the chosen implementation did under observed conditions. Unmodelled effects cannot be conjured into a runtime by disclaimer alone.

## 8. Practical direction and falsifiability
Keep the prototype and empirical checks. Test whether KIP's participation **actually alters material locally available possibilities** in a manner not wholly dictated by a hardcoded expected answer. Preserve unknowns and adverse results. Do not pre-populate the relational field with radiator/repair, safety/success, apple/sugar, engineer/arrival or 5D shield correct-answer labels. Such examples are diagnostic objections, **not semantic test oracles**.

Evidence gates **claims**, not all construction. Build in authorised isolation; scope any positive conclusion narrowly. A failure is informative when it can distinguish 'not implemented' from 'proved impossible'.
